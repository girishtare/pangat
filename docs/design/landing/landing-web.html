400 cards go out. 700 to 800 people are expected. Nobody knows the real number until the day.

The caterer needs one number weeks before the wedding. Order too much and food and money go to waste; order too little and the hosts are embarrassed. Pangat replaces weeks of phone calls with a reply from every family.

How it works

Three steps. One honest number.

1

Send each family its own link

Load your guest list from Excel. Pangat opens WhatsApp with the message already written, and prints a QR slip for every card.

2

They reply in a minute

Haldi, wedding, reception: who is coming to each, how many, and how many eat vegetarian. No sign-up, big buttons, Marathi first.

3

You see the real number

Live headcount per function, split veg and non-veg, plus an expected count that allows for families who haven't replied yet.
